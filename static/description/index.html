Repair Shop Tickets

A complete ticket management system built for repair & service shops

Key Features

Ticket Workflow

Track every repair job through a clear, controlled workflow:
New → In Progress ↔ Waiting for Parts → Resolved → Ready for Pickup → Picked Up

Cancel is available at any active stage with a confirmation dialog. Reopen a resolved job if the repair fails — stock is automatically reversed.

Parts & Labor

Add parts lines with automatic price lookup from your product catalog. Set a separate labor price. The running total is always visible and updates instantly.

When a ticket is resolved, parts are automatically deducted from warehouse stock. Cancelling or reopening the ticket reverses the stock move.

Invoice in One Click

Generate a customer invoice directly from the ticket. Parts and labor are copied automatically, with VAT applied dynamically from your tax configuration.

Bilingual Thermal Receipt

Print a professional 80mm thermal receipt. If the customer's language is Greek, the entire receipt prints in Greek — including labels, totals, and full terms & conditions. English otherwise.

Customer & Technician Notes

Two separate note fields on every ticket: one for customer-facing observations and one for internal technician use. Neither appears on the printed receipt.

Brands & Models

Maintain a catalog of device brands and models. Filter and group your tickets by device for instant statistics and daily overviews.

Chatter & Activities

Log internal notes, send messages, and schedule follow-up activities — all from the ticket form using Odoo's standard chatter.

Greek Translation

Full Greek (el) translation included for all menus, fields, buttons, status labels, and the printed receipt.
