Repair Shop Tickets

A complete ticket management system built for repair shops

Features

Ticket Workflow

Track every repair job through a clear, controlled workflow: New → In Progress → Waiting for Parts → Resolved → Ready for Pickup. No more lost jobs or forgotten repairs.

Parts & Labor

Add parts lines with automatic price lookup from your product catalog. Set a separate labor price. The total is always calculated and visible.

📋 Brands & Models

Maintain a catalog of device brands and models. Filter and group your tickets by device for instant statistics.

📄 Invoice in One Click

Generate a customer invoice directly from the ticket — parts and labor are copied automatically, with 24% VAT applied.

🧾 80mm Thermal Receipt

Print a professional 80mm thermal receipt with your company name, job details, itemized parts, totals, and full terms & conditions.

💬 Chatter & Notes

Log internal notes, send messages to customers, schedule activities — all from the ticket form using Odoo's standard chatter.

🔍 Powerful Search

Filter by status, brand, job type or customer. Group by any field for quick daily overviews. Kanban view included.

🇬🇷 Greek Translation

Full Greek (el) translation included for all menus, fields, buttons, and the printed receipt.

Workflow Stages

New → In Progress ↔ Waiting for Parts → Resolved → Ready for Pickup

Cancel available at any stage. Reopen (Repair Failed) available from Resolved and Ready for Pickup.
